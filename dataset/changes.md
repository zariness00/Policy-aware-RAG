# Policy version changes

## P01-v1 → P01-v2

- **Scope:** This policy applies to all employees of XYZ Corporation who are required to travel for business purposes. It covers all aspects of travel booking, accommodation arrangements, and the reimbursement process for travel-related expenses incurred during business trips. Additionally, it now includes contractors and temporary staff who may travel on behalf of the company.
- **Requirements:** Employees must submit a travel request form at least 21 days prior to the intended travel date for approval by their direct supervisor. All travel expenses must be pre-approved, and any expenses exceeding $750 must receive additional approval from the department head.

## P02-v1 → P02-v2

- **Requirements:** Employees must submit their expense claims within 45 days of incurring the expense to ensure timely processing. All claims must include original receipts, a completed expense claim form, and a brief description of the business purpose for each expense, with amounts not exceeding the company’s established limits for each category, such as $50 for meals and $200 for lodging per night.
- **Exceptions:** Exceptions to this policy may be granted in special circumstances, such as extended travel or unforeseen expenses. Employees seeking an exception must submit a written request to their direct supervisor for approval, which must be documented and retained for audit purposes. Additionally, exceptions may now be granted for expenses exceeding the established limits if prior approval is obtained from the finance department.

## P03-v1 → P03-v2

- **Exceptions:** Exceptions to this policy may be granted in cases of emergency procurement, where immediate action is necessary to prevent operational disruption. In such cases, the employee must notify their department head and submit a post-purchase justification within 24 hours for review and potential approval.
- **Procedure:** Employees must submit a Purchase Request Form to their department head for any intended purchase. The department head will review the request and, if approved, forward it to the Finance Department for purchases over $2,500, which will be processed within five business days of receipt.

## P03-v2 → P03-v3

- **Scope:** This policy applies to all employees involved in the procurement process, including but not limited to department heads, project managers, and administrative staff. It covers all purchases made on behalf of the company, regardless of the payment method or vendor, and now includes contractors and consultants engaged in procurement activities.
- **Requirements:** All purchases exceeding $750 must receive prior approval from the respective department head. Additionally, any purchase above $3,000 requires a secondary approval from the Finance Department to ensure budget compliance and financial oversight.

## P04-v1 → P04-v2

- **Procedure:** The onboarding process begins with the completion of the Supplier Evaluation Form, followed by a review by the procurement team. Once the evaluation is complete, the procurement team will present their findings to the Supplier Approval Committee, which will make a decision within 10 business days. If approved, a formal contract will be drafted and sent to the supplier for signature.
- **Records:** All documentation related to the supplier onboarding process, including the Supplier Evaluation Form, reference checks, and approval records, must be maintained for a minimum of seven years. These records will be stored in the company’s secure document management system and will be accessible for audits and compliance reviews as needed.

## P05-v1 → P05-v2

- **Records:** Old Rule: Records will be retained for a minimum of three years. 
New Rule: Records will be retained for a minimum of five years to enhance long-term analysis and trend identification.
- **Scope:** Old Rule: This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors who have access to the company’s information systems and data. 
New Rule: This policy applies to all employees, contractors, third-party vendors, and any other individuals who interact with the company’s information systems and data.

## P06-v1 → P06-v2

- **Scope:** This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors who require access to the company's IT systems and data. It encompasses all digital resources, including databases, applications, and network infrastructure. The scope will now also include temporary staff and interns who may require access for specific projects.
- **Requirements:** All access requests must be submitted through the IT Access Request Form and require approval from the employee's direct supervisor and the IT Manager. Access must be reviewed at least every six months to ensure that it remains appropriate based on the user's role and responsibilities. The review period will now be changed to every three months for high-risk roles to enhance security oversight.

## P06-v2 → P06-v3

- **Exceptions:** Exceptions to this policy may be granted in cases where immediate access is required for business continuity, such as during system outages. In such cases, the request must be documented and approved by the IT Director within 12 hours of access being granted.
- **Procedure:** To initiate an access request, employees must complete the IT Access Request Form and submit it to their supervisor for approval. Once approved, the request will be forwarded to the IT department, which will process the request within two business days and notify the employee of their access status.

## P07-v1 → P07-v2

- **Requirements:** Employees must submit a Remote Work Request Form at least one week prior to the desired remote work date. Approval from both the direct supervisor and the HR department is required, and employees must demonstrate that their job responsibilities can be effectively managed from a remote location.
- **Exceptions:** Exceptions to this policy may be granted for employees with special circumstances, such as medical needs or family emergencies. Such requests must be documented and submitted to HR for review, and approval must be obtained from the department head and HR within three business days.

## P08-v1 → P08-v2

- **Exceptions:** Old Rule: Exceptions to the retention schedule may be granted only with written approval from the department head and must be documented in the records management system. New Rule: Exceptions to the retention schedule may be granted with written approval from the department head or the Records Management Officer and must be documented in the records management system.
- **Procedure:** Old Rule: Employees must submit a request for record disposal to their immediate supervisor at least 30 days prior to the scheduled disposal date. New Rule: Employees must submit a request for record disposal to their immediate supervisor at least 14 days prior to the scheduled disposal date.

## P09-v1 → P09-v2

- **Procedure:** Change the notification timeline for training deadlines from 14 days to 21 days prior to the training deadline, with reminders still sent at 7 days and 1 day before the due date.
- **Records:** Update the frequency of training status reports provided to supervisors from quarterly to monthly.

## P09-v2 → P09-v3

- **Scope:** This policy applies to all employees of the company, including full-time, part-time, and temporary staff. It encompasses all mandatory training programs identified by the company, including safety, compliance, and job-specific training modules, as well as any additional training deemed necessary by department heads.
- **Requirements:** All employees must complete mandatory training within 45 days of their start date or as specified by their department head. Employees are required to complete a minimum of 15 hours of professional development training annually to maintain eligibility for performance bonuses and promotions.

## P10-v1 → P10-v2

- **Records:** Change the retention period for induction records from 'a minimum of three years' to 'a minimum of five years'.
- **Scope:** Expand the scope to include all vendors and service providers who require access to any company site, in addition to contractors and subcontractors.

## P11-v1 → P11-v2

- **Scope:** This policy applies to all employees who operate, manage, or oversee company equipment, including machinery, vehicles, and electronic devices. It encompasses all equipment owned or leased by the company, regardless of its location or usage context. **New Rule:** The policy now includes all contractors and third-party service providers who interact with company equipment, expanding the scope to ensure comprehensive maintenance oversight.
- **Requirements:** All employees must conduct a visual inspection of their assigned equipment at the beginning of each shift and complete a maintenance checklist. Any identified issues must be reported to the Equipment Maintenance Coordinator within 24 hours, and a formal maintenance request must be submitted for any repairs needed exceeding $100 in estimated costs. **New Rule:** The deadline for reporting identified issues has been changed from 24 hours to 12 hours to enhance responsiveness and minimize equipment downtime.

## P12-v1 → P12-v2

- **Requirements:** All customer complaints must be acknowledged within 12 hours of receipt. Employees are required to document the complaint details in the Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system, including the customer's name, contact information, and a summary of the issue, within 24 hours of acknowledgment.
- **Exceptions:** Complaints that are deemed frivolous or abusive may be escalated to the Customer Service Manager for review. In such cases, the employee must provide a detailed report justifying the classification of the complaint as frivolous or abusive, which will be reviewed within 48 hours.

## P12-v2 → P12-v3

- **Scope:** This policy applies to all employees of XYZ Corporation who interact with customers, including customer service representatives, sales staff, and management. It covers all forms of customer complaints, whether received via phone, email, or in-person interactions. **The scope now includes third-party vendors who handle customer interactions on behalf of XYZ Corporation.**
- **Procedure:** Upon receiving a complaint, the employee must first acknowledge it to the customer and log it in the CRM system. If the complaint cannot be resolved within 5 business days, it must be escalated to the Customer Service Manager, who will have an additional 5 business days to address the issue and communicate the resolution to the customer. **The procedure now requires that all escalated complaints must be reviewed by the Customer Service Director if unresolved after 10 business days.**
