# Employee Training Policy

Document type: Release notes
Policy ID: P09
Version ID: P09-v3
Effective from: 2026-01-01
Changes from: P09-v2

## Changes

- Scope: Mandatory training now also includes additional training deemed necessary by department heads.
- Training requirements: The standard completion period after an employee's start date increased from 30 days to 45 days. The annual professional development minimum increased from 10 hours to 15 hours.
