# Procurement Approval Policy

Document type: Release notes
Policy ID: P03
Version ID: P03-v2
Effective from: 2025-01-01
Changes from: P03-v1

## Changes

- Emergency procurement: The deadline for submitting a post-purchase justification decreased from 48 hours to 24 hours.
- Purchase requests: The Finance Department's processing time increased from three business days to five business days after receipt.
