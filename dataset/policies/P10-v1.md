# Contractor Site Access Policy

Policy ID: P10
Version ID: P10-v1
Effective from: 2024-01-01
Supersedes: None

## Purpose

The purpose of the Contractor Site Access Policy is to ensure that all contractors are properly inducted and authorized before accessing company premises. This policy aims to maintain a safe and secure working environment while ensuring compliance with legal and regulatory requirements.

## Scope

This policy applies to all contractors, subcontractors, and their employees who require access to any company site. It encompasses all types of work, including maintenance, construction, and consultancy services.

## Requirements

All contractors must complete a site induction training session prior to accessing any company premises. This training must be completed at least 48 hours before the scheduled start date of their work, and a record of completion must be submitted to the site manager for approval.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy may be granted in emergency situations where immediate access is required to prevent harm or damage. In such cases, the contractor must notify the site manager immediately and complete the induction training within 24 hours of access.

## Procedure

Contractors must submit a request for site access to the site manager at least five business days prior to the intended start date. The site manager will review the request, verify the contractor's qualifications, and schedule the induction training session, which must be attended by all personnel requiring access.

## Records

Records of all completed inductions, including the names of attendees and dates of training, must be maintained by the site manager for a minimum of three years. Additionally, access logs must be kept to track contractor movements on site, which will be reviewed quarterly for compliance.
