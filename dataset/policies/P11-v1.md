# Equipment Maintenance Policy

Policy ID: P11
Version ID: P11-v1
Effective from: 2024-01-01
Supersedes: None

## Purpose

The purpose of the Equipment Maintenance Policy is to ensure that all company equipment is regularly inspected, maintained, and any faults are reported promptly to minimize downtime and enhance operational efficiency. This policy aims to establish clear guidelines for employees regarding their responsibilities in maintaining equipment safety and functionality.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees who operate, manage, or oversee company equipment, including machinery, vehicles, and electronic devices. It encompasses all equipment owned or leased by the company, regardless of its location or usage context.

## Requirements

All employees must conduct a visual inspection of their assigned equipment at the beginning of each shift and complete a maintenance checklist. Any identified issues must be reported to the Equipment Maintenance Coordinator within 24 hours, and a formal maintenance request must be submitted for any repairs needed exceeding $100 in estimated costs.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy may be granted in cases of emergency equipment failure, where immediate action is required to prevent operational disruption. In such cases, employees must notify their supervisor and the Equipment Maintenance Coordinator as soon as possible, and a post-incident report must be submitted within 48 hours detailing the circumstances and actions taken.

## Procedure

Employees must complete the Equipment Maintenance Checklist and submit it to their supervisor at the end of each week. If any equipment requires maintenance, the employee must fill out a Maintenance Request Form, which must be approved by the Equipment Maintenance Coordinator before any work can commence, with a maximum approval turnaround time of 48 hours.

## Records

All maintenance requests and inspection checklists must be documented and stored in the Equipment Maintenance Database for a minimum of three years. The Equipment Maintenance Coordinator is responsible for ensuring that records are updated and accessible for audits and reviews, and must conduct quarterly reviews of maintenance logs to identify recurring issues or trends.
