# Remote Work Policy

Policy ID: P07
Version ID: P07-v1
Effective from: 2024-01-01
Supersedes: None

## Purpose

The purpose of this Remote Work Policy is to establish clear guidelines for employees regarding eligibility, scheduling, and approval processes for remote work arrangements. This policy aims to enhance productivity while ensuring that team collaboration and communication remain effective.

## Scope

This policy applies to all full-time employees of the company who have completed their probationary period. Part-time employees and contractors may be considered for remote work on a case-by-case basis, subject to managerial discretion.

## Requirements

Employees must submit a Remote Work Request Form at least two weeks prior to the desired remote work date. Approval from both the direct supervisor and the HR department is required, and employees must demonstrate that their job responsibilities can be effectively managed from a remote location.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy may be granted for employees with special circumstances, such as medical needs or family emergencies. Such requests must be documented and submitted to HR for review, and approval must be obtained from the department head and HR within five business days.

## Procedure

To initiate a remote work request, employees must complete the Remote Work Request Form and submit it to their supervisor. Supervisors will review the request within three business days and communicate their decision to the employee, who must then forward the approved request to HR for final approval.

## Records

All approved remote work requests will be documented and stored in the employee's personnel file. Supervisors are responsible for maintaining records of remote work schedules and ensuring that employees adhere to their agreed-upon work hours, which must align with the company's core operating hours of 9 AM to 5 PM.
