# IT Access Review Policy

Policy ID: P06
Version ID: P06-v2
Effective from: 2025-01-01
Supersedes: P06-v1

## Purpose

The purpose of the IT Access Review Policy is to establish a framework for granting, monitoring, and regularly reviewing access to company systems and data. This policy aims to ensure that access is granted based on the principle of least privilege and that it is regularly reviewed to mitigate security risks.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, third-party vendors, temporary staff, and interns who require access to the company's IT systems and data. It encompasses all digital resources, including databases, applications, and network infrastructure.

## Requirements

All access requests must be submitted through the IT Access Request Form and require approval from the employee's direct supervisor and the IT Manager. Access must be reviewed at least every three months for high-risk roles and at least every six months for all other roles to ensure that it remains appropriate based on the user's role and responsibilities.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy may be granted in cases where immediate access is required for business continuity, such as during system outages. In such cases, the request must be documented and approved by the IT Director within 24 hours of access being granted.

## Procedure

To initiate an access request, employees must complete the IT Access Request Form and submit it to their supervisor for approval. Once approved, the request will be forwarded to the IT department, which will process the request within three business days and notify the employee of their access status.

## Records

All access requests and reviews must be documented and stored in the IT Access Management System for a minimum of three years. The IT department is responsible for maintaining these records and ensuring they are available for audit purposes upon request.
