# Employee Training Policy

Policy ID: P09
Version ID: P09-v2
Effective from: 2025-01-01
Supersedes: P09-v1

## Purpose

The purpose of this Employee Training Policy is to ensure that all employees receive the necessary training to perform their roles effectively and to maintain compliance with industry standards. This policy outlines the requirements for mandatory training, the process for tracking overdue training, and the consequences of non-compliance.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees of the company, including full-time, part-time, and temporary staff. It encompasses all mandatory training programs identified by the company, including safety, compliance, and job-specific training modules.

## Requirements

All employees must complete mandatory training within 30 days of their start date or as specified by their department head. Employees are required to complete a minimum of 10 hours of professional development training annually to maintain eligibility for performance bonuses and promotions.

## Exceptions

Employees may request an extension for mandatory training due to extenuating circumstances, such as medical leave or personal emergencies. Such requests must be submitted in writing to the HR department at least 5 business days before the training deadline and will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

## Procedure

Employees will receive notifications via email 21 days prior to the training deadline, with reminders sent at 7 days and 1 day before the due date. If training is not completed by the deadline, the employee's supervisor will be notified, and a meeting will be scheduled to discuss the reasons for non-compliance and potential next steps.

## Records

HR will maintain a training database that tracks completion rates for all mandatory training programs. Supervisors will receive monthly reports on their team's training status, and employees will have access to their individual training records through the company intranet.

## Revision notes

Changes from P09-v1:

- Change the notification timeline for training deadlines from 14 days to 21 days prior to the training deadline, with reminders still sent at 7 days and 1 day before the due date.
- Update the frequency of training status reports provided to supervisors from quarterly to monthly.
