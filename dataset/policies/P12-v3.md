# Customer Complaint Policy

Policy ID: P12
Version ID: P12-v3
Effective from: 2026-01-01
Supersedes: P12-v2

## Purpose

The purpose of this Customer Complaint Policy is to establish a clear and efficient process for acknowledging, escalating, and resolving customer complaints. This policy aims to enhance customer satisfaction and improve service quality by ensuring that all complaints are handled promptly and effectively.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees of XYZ Corporation who interact with customers, including customer service representatives, sales staff, and management. It covers all forms of customer complaints, whether received via phone, email, or in-person interactions. The scope now includes third-party vendors who handle customer interactions on behalf of XYZ Corporation.

## Requirements

All customer complaints must be acknowledged within 12 hours of receipt. Employees are required to document the complaint details in the Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system, including the customer's name, contact information, and a summary of the issue, within 24 hours of acknowledgment.

## Exceptions

Complaints that are deemed frivolous or abusive may be escalated to the Customer Service Manager for review. In such cases, the employee must provide a detailed report justifying the classification of the complaint as frivolous or abusive, which will be reviewed within 48 hours.

## Procedure

Upon receiving a complaint, the employee must first acknowledge it to the customer and log it in the CRM system. If the complaint cannot be resolved within 5 business days, it must be escalated to the Customer Service Manager, who will have an additional 5 business days to address the issue and communicate the resolution to the customer. The procedure now requires that all escalated complaints must be reviewed by the Customer Service Director if unresolved after 10 business days.

## Records

All complaints and their resolutions must be recorded in the CRM system for tracking and analysis purposes. Records should be maintained for a minimum of 2 years, and quarterly reports summarizing complaint trends and resolutions must be submitted to the Quality Assurance team for review.
