# Travel and Accommodation Policy

Policy ID: P01
Version ID: P01-v1
Effective from: 2024-01-01
Supersedes: None

## Purpose

The purpose of this Travel and Accommodation Policy is to establish clear guidelines for employees regarding the booking and reimbursement of business travel expenses. This policy aims to ensure that all travel is conducted in a cost-effective manner while maintaining employee safety and comfort.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees of XYZ Corporation who are required to travel for business purposes. It covers all aspects of travel booking, accommodation arrangements, and the reimbursement process for travel-related expenses incurred during business trips.

## Requirements

Employees must submit a travel request form at least 14 days prior to the intended travel date for approval by their direct supervisor. All travel expenses must be pre-approved, and any expenses exceeding $500 must receive additional approval from the department head.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy may be granted in cases of urgent business needs or unforeseen circumstances. Employees seeking an exception must submit a written request to the HR department, which will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis and must be approved by the Chief Financial Officer (CFO).

## Procedure

To initiate travel arrangements, employees must complete the travel request form and submit it to their supervisor for approval. Once approved, employees can book travel through the designated corporate travel agency, and all bookings must be made at least 7 days in advance to ensure the best rates.

## Records

All travel requests and expense reimbursements must be documented and submitted to the finance department within 30 days of the completion of travel. Employees are required to retain all receipts and submit them along with their expense report, which must include a detailed breakdown of expenses and the purpose of the trip.
