# Expense Claims Policy

Policy ID: P02
Version ID: P02-v1
Effective from: 2024-01-01
Supersedes: None

## Purpose

The purpose of the Expense Claims Policy is to establish clear guidelines for employees regarding the submission and approval of expense claims incurred during business activities. This policy aims to ensure that all expenses are reasonable, necessary, and properly documented to maintain financial integrity and accountability within the organization.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees of the company who incur expenses while performing their job duties. It covers all types of expenses, including travel, meals, lodging, and other business-related costs, and is applicable to both full-time and part-time employees.

## Requirements

Employees must submit their expense claims within 30 days of incurring the expense to ensure timely processing. All claims must include original receipts, a completed expense claim form, and a brief description of the business purpose for each expense, with amounts not exceeding the company’s established limits for each category, such as $50 for meals and $200 for lodging per night.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy may be granted in special circumstances, such as extended travel or unforeseen expenses. Employees seeking an exception must submit a written request to their direct supervisor for approval, which must be documented and retained for audit purposes.

## Procedure

To submit an expense claim, employees must complete the designated expense claim form and attach all required documentation. Claims must be submitted to the employee's direct supervisor for approval, who has five business days to review and either approve or deny the claim; if denied, the supervisor must provide a written explanation to the employee.

## Records

All approved expense claims must be retained in the company’s financial records for a minimum of three years. The finance department will conduct quarterly audits of expense claims to ensure compliance with this policy and will report any discrepancies to the management team for further action.
