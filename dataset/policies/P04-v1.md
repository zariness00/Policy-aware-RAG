# Supplier Onboarding Policy

Policy ID: P04
Version ID: P04-v1
Effective from: 2024-01-01
Supersedes: None

## Purpose

The purpose of the Supplier Onboarding Policy is to establish a standardized process for evaluating and approving new suppliers to ensure they meet the company's quality, compliance, and ethical standards. This policy aims to mitigate risks associated with supplier relationships and to promote transparency and accountability in the procurement process.

## Scope

This policy applies to all departments within the company that engage with external suppliers for goods and services. It encompasses all potential suppliers, regardless of their size or the nature of the products or services they provide, and is applicable to both domestic and international suppliers.

## Requirements

All new suppliers must complete a Supplier Evaluation Form, which includes details about their financial stability, compliance with industry regulations, and ethical practices. Additionally, suppliers must provide at least three references from current or past clients, which will be verified by the procurement team within 10 business days of submission.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy may be granted in cases of emergency procurement, where immediate supplier engagement is necessary to prevent operational disruptions. In such cases, the department head must submit a written request to the Chief Procurement Officer (CPO) for approval, which must be obtained within 48 hours of the emergency situation arising.

## Procedure

The onboarding process begins with the completion of the Supplier Evaluation Form, followed by a review by the procurement team. Once the evaluation is complete, the procurement team will present their findings to the Supplier Approval Committee, which will make a decision within 15 business days. If approved, a formal contract will be drafted and sent to the supplier for signature.

## Records

All documentation related to the supplier onboarding process, including the Supplier Evaluation Form, reference checks, and approval records, must be maintained for a minimum of five years. These records will be stored in the company’s secure document management system and will be accessible for audits and compliance reviews as needed.
