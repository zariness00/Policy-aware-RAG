# Security Incident Policy

Policy ID: P05
Version ID: P05-v1
Effective from: 2024-01-01
Supersedes: None

## Purpose

The purpose of the Security Incident Policy is to establish a clear framework for reporting, managing, and escalating suspected security incidents within the organization. This policy aims to protect company assets, data integrity, and the overall security posture of the organization by ensuring timely and effective responses to potential threats.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors who have access to the company’s information systems and data. It encompasses all suspected security incidents, including but not limited to data breaches, unauthorized access, malware infections, and phishing attempts.

## Requirements

All employees must report any suspected security incident to the IT Security Team within 24 hours of detection. Reports should include a detailed description of the incident, the time of occurrence, and any actions taken prior to reporting. Failure to report incidents in a timely manner may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy may be granted only by the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) in cases where reporting may compromise ongoing investigations or where the incident is deemed to be a false alarm. Any exceptions must be documented and reviewed during the next quarterly security meeting to ensure compliance and understanding of the policy.

## Procedure

Upon receiving a report of a suspected security incident, the IT Security Team will initiate an investigation within 48 hours. The team will categorize the incident based on severity and potential impact, escalating to senior management if the incident is classified as high-risk. Employees are required to cooperate fully with the investigation and provide any necessary information or access to systems as requested.

## Records

All reported incidents and their outcomes will be documented in the Security Incident Log, which will be maintained by the IT Security Team. This log will include the date of the incident, the nature of the incident, actions taken, and the final resolution. Records will be retained for a minimum of three years and will be reviewed annually to identify trends and improve security measures.
