# Procurement Approval Policy

Policy ID: P03
Version ID: P03-v3
Effective from: 2026-01-01
Supersedes: P03-v2

## Purpose

The purpose of the Procurement Approval Policy is to establish clear guidelines for the approval of purchases and the delegation of spending authority within the company. This policy aims to ensure that all procurement activities are conducted in a transparent, efficient, and accountable manner, minimizing the risk of unauthorized spending.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees involved in the procurement process, including but not limited to department heads, project managers, administrative staff, contractors, and consultants engaged in procurement activities. It covers all purchases made on behalf of the company, regardless of the payment method or vendor.

## Requirements

All purchases exceeding $750 must receive prior approval from the respective department head. Additionally, any purchase above $3,000 requires a secondary approval from the Finance Department to ensure budget compliance and financial oversight.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy may be granted in cases of emergency procurement, where immediate action is necessary to prevent operational disruption. In such cases, the employee must notify their department head and submit a post-purchase justification within 24 hours for review and potential approval.

## Procedure

Employees must submit a Purchase Request Form to their department head for any intended purchase. The department head will review the request and, if approved, forward it to the Finance Department for purchases over $3,000, which will be processed within five business days of receipt.

## Records

All approved Purchase Request Forms must be retained for a minimum of three years in the department's procurement records. The Finance Department will maintain a centralized log of all purchases exceeding $3,000 for auditing purposes, which will be reviewed quarterly to ensure compliance with this policy.
